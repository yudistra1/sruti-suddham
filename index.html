What to work on

Just intonation is the default because that is what a tanpura enforces. Against a drone, Ga₃ sits at 5:4 — about 386¢ above Sa, a good 14¢ below where a fretted guitar puts it. Ni₃ at 15:8 is 12¢ flat of equal temperament. If your ear is set by the guitar, switch above and watch the two targets move.

Wear headphones. The tanpura leaking back into the mic will pull the needle toward Sa and Pa. Echo cancellation is on, but it is not perfect on open speakers. If macOS has grabbed your iPhone as the input, pick the built-in mic from the dropdown above.

Sarali Varisai is set in Mayamalavagowla — S R₁ G₃ M₁ P D₁ N₃ Ṡ — the scale beginners are given first because its symmetric semitones train the ear on both sides of the tetrachord. Pitch tracked by normalised autocorrelation; tanpura synthesised by Karplus–Strong.
